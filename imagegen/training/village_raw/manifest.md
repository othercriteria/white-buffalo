# Training set manifest: Plains camp on the move with travois

Concept LoRA raw acquisitions, 2026-07-09. Target register: 1850s monochrome
engraving. Engravings/aquatints preferred; oils/watercolors to be grayscaled.
Photographs (3, flagged) are pose reference only — last-resort register.

All works verified public domain: every artist died before 1956 and every
print source was published before 1930. Museum-photo sources are CC0 or
"no known restrictions."

## Engravings / prints (monochrome, native register)

### catlin-letters-notes-plate21-sioux-moving-camp.jpg
- Title: Plates 21 & 22, *Letters and Notes on the Manners, Customs, and Condition of the North American Indians*, Vol. 1 (top panel: Sioux/Crow moving camp)
- Artist: George Catlin (1796-1872)
- Date: published 1841 (London)
- Source: https://archive.org/download/illustrationsofm01catl/page/n88.jpg (Internet Archive scan, UC Libraries copy)
- Resolution: 2041x3100
- Medium: line engraving (book plate)
- PD rationale: published 1841; artist d. 1872
- Note: Two panels on one page. Top (pl. 21): moving camp — horse travois with women/children riding the pole load, poles dragging in clear triangles, dog travois alongside. Bottom (pl. 22): pitched camp with meat-drying racks. Crop panels before training.
