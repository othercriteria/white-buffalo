# Farrell reference (contrast anchor)

Purpose (DK, 2026-07-08): a drafted Farrell even without a full image
series, as contrast while individuating Morrow. Same discipline as
morrow-reference.md: "Canon" is cited; "Reference choices" are
underdetermined-by-text choices fixed for visual consistency only.

## Canon (visual-bible §Farrell)

- "Medium height, lean, with a face that had been weathered past his
  years" (01:69); pale eyes, "color difficult to determine" (01:75);
  calloused hands, scarred knuckles (01:89). ~32 in Nov 1858 (enlisted at
  twenty, 03:61).
- Beard on the trail — freezes solid (07:21, 11:7). Hat (07:85); boots
  (01:69); coat "worn but adequate" (01:69), then "a heavy coat" bought
  for the journey (03:43).
- Rifle "carried since the war," muzzleloader, rifle case/scabbard
  (03:43, 03:63, 11:65). The withdrawal arc is visible late (tremor,
  sweating, 13:5 →) — reference look predates the collapse.
- [gap] hair, beard color, any face detail beyond Ch 1's sketch.

## Reference choices (proposed — contrast axis vs Morrow)

- Build: compact and contained where Morrow is tall and attenuated;
  squared shoulders, economical stance.
- Hair: light brown/sandy, cropped short (vs Morrow's long dark lank);
  suits the Scots-Irish Pennsylvania background and reads distinct at
  plate resolution.
- Beard: short rough trail beard, weeks not years (vs chest-length).
- Face: broad, level brow; lines at the eye corners doing the "weathered
  past his years" work; watchful, measuring expression (vs Morrow's
  far-off gaze). Pale eyes render naturally in engraving as light iris.
- Kit when shown: broad-brimmed low felt hat, heavy dark winter coat,
  rifle in scabbard or crooked in arm — the armed, provisioned man
  against Morrow's "no gun, only a knife."

## Anchors (DK, 2026-07-08)

s701 = Farrell BEFORE the journey (fuller face, composed, folded hands);
s702 = Farrell AFTER, if he survives (hollowed, harder, the trail's cost
in the face) — "which is sort of the conceit of the text's existence,
even if the text is coy about it." Any Farrell plate should pick its
moment on that axis: pre-departure scenes read s701, post-homestead
scenes drift toward s702. The withdrawal arc (visual-bible §Farrell)
gives the interpolation.
