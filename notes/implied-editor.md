# The implied editor — modal reading (DK, 2026-07-17; NEVER ENACT)

STATUS: On-record fanon. This is the authors' modal reconstruction
of how the book-as-object exists in its own fiction. It is NOT
canon to be written toward, referenced in text, or "fixed" against.
The compiler question is certified productive-UNSETTLED (two
independent facilitator runs: "the book means for the question to
be live"); rival readings (e.g., Farrell as sole arranger) remain
equally licensed. Do not spell any of this out in the text.

THE MODAL READING: Farrell lives, by the mechanisms the text
stages (horse, stocked homestead, money owed, past-tense
narration). He returns east with the journal but NOT Catherine's
letters (Ch 6: drafts returned to the box, lid closed; Ch 11:
"her letters in their box" inventoried as staying). He gives
Aldridge a somewhat thorough report and produces a rough-hewn
treatment himself (or dictates it). A later editor — possibly
Aldridge himself, decades on — puts it into literary form: the
interleaving of account and journal, the redaction of the murder
entry (propriety; period-true), the commissioning of plates in
the period engraving idiom.

TEXTUAL SUPPORTS:
- "From the Journal — I" headers: the selection was always
  announced; the excerpting hand is already in the apparatus.
- Document sorting is staged in-text (journal in saddlebag,
  letters left). Quoted drafts = Farrell's memory-mediation,
  like the whole account.
- CH 1 IS THE EDITOR'S FINGERPRINT: the "ownerless" third-person
  chapter (hostile Fable, sweep 3) is stocked exclusively with
  interior Aldridge could supply (rehearsals, second-best coat,
  unsent letter). The book opens inside the commissioning father
  because the commission was his. Finding dissolved: anomaly ->
  signature.
- The redaction acquires a hand with period motives (the appendix
  analysis's insight, no appendix needed). If the editor is
  Aldridge: her father is the hand keeping Catherine's death off
  the page — the Catherine's-privacy ground enforced in-fiction.
- Polish objection: many cooks, one plater — the literary form is
  a single late hand.

KNOWN SEAM (accepted): verbatim quotation of the crossed-out
draft despite the letters staying west — memory-mediation; the
club's most affecting passage is, under this reading, Farrell's
recollection. Acceptable and arguably improving.
